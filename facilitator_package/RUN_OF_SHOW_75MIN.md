# THUNDERFLUNKED 2.0 — 75-Minute Run-of-Show (authoritative)

**Audience:** ~50–60 faculty · **Teams:** ~10–12 tables of 5 · **Devices:** one shared
laptop per team + the GM laptop + a projector. This is the **current, authoritative**
facilitator script and matches the **fully digital** build: roles, allegiance, and
secret missions are revealed *on the team laptop* (there are **no physical role,
allegiance, or mission cards to deal**), and sabotage surfaces as **hallway rumors**
you choose when to announce.

> **Official target: ~75 minutes.** This is the comfortable, recommended pace — it
> keeps **every** feature (all five checkpoints, all three private rounds, all five
> teaching moments, sabotage, hallway rumors, accusations, and the full final reveal)
> and leaves real room for faculty discussion and Canvas exploration. A hard-capped
> 60-minute version is possible by time-boxing, but 75 is the better experience.

**Legend:** `[SAY]` read/paraphrase aloud · `[GM]` button on the `/gm` screen ·
`[TABLE]` what the five faculty do on their laptop · `[PROJECTOR]` what the room
screen shows · `⏱` target clock.

---

## PRE-EVENT (before the 75 minutes start)

**T-30 min (set up the room):**
- Start the server (`README_RUN_GAME.md`), open **`/gm`** on your laptop and
  **`/projector`** on the room screen (separate window, dragged to the projector).
- On `/gm`, click **Create New Game**. Write the 5-character **game code** on the
  board. Everything you control lives on `/gm`.
- Confirm every table laptop is on the room Wi-Fi with **`/team`** open.
- Lay out the **still-physical** reference decks at each table — **Course Cards,
  Student Cards, and the Resource & Support (COURSE TOOL / STUDENT SUPPORT) deck**.
  *(Roles, allegiance, and secret missions are digital — nothing to deal.)*

**T-10 min (pre-stage joining):**
- As faculty arrive, have each table **enter the game code + a team name and add
  their 5 player names**, then wait at the roles step. This keeps the opening
  relaxed instead of 50+ people typing against the clock.

**At 0:00:** `[GM]` **Start Session Timer** on `/gm` — the pacing panel will show
*Ahead / On pace / Behind* against this plan for the rest of the hour.

---

## ⏱ 0:00–0:04 — Welcome & framing (4 min)
- `[SAY]` "You are an instructional team guiding one Canvas course through a
  semester. Your job: get your students **and** your course through the term with
  good course-design **process** — not by spotting 'bad students.'"
- `[SAY]` "One person at each table is secretly **Thunderflunked** and wants the
  course to fail — mostly by being persuasive. You won't catch them on vibes; you
  catch **bad ideas with good process.**"
- `[SAY]` "Everything private happens on your table's laptop. When it says *pass
  the laptop* and *look away*, honor it — that's the whole game."
- `[PROJECTOR]` Room status / course assignments.

## ⏱ 0:04–0:09 — Private roles, allegiance & secret missions (5 min)
- `[TABLE]` Shuffle & confirm the table's public roles, then **pass the laptop one
  player at a time**: each person privately sees their **allegiance**
  (Thunderstruck / Thunderflunked) **and their Secret Mission** on the same screen,
  taps *"I have seen it — hide and pass on,"* and passes.
- `[SAY]` "Keep your allegiance and your mission secret all game. Missions are a
  small bonus at the end; they don't decide who wins. You can privately re-check
  your mission later during any checkpoint."
- `[GM]` Watch the command center fill in as tables finish.

## ⏱ 0:09–0:14 — Course selection & student roster (5 min)
- `[GM]` **Open Course Selection** → tables review their dealt **Course Card** and
  may propose one 1-for-1 trade (completes only if both tables accept).
  `[GM]` **Close Course Selection** to lock courses.
- `[TABLE]` On the placement screen, **keep 10 of the 15 dealt students**; the other
  5 are referred. No written rationale needed.
- `[GM]` Under **Student Reassignment**, click **Distribute Replacements** once
  tables confirm; tables then acknowledge their final 15-student class.
- `[SAY]` "Your roster nudges your starting conditions a little — it does **not**
  decide your outcome. Your decisions do."

## THE FIVE CHECKPOINTS — the repeating rhythm

Every checkpoint has the same four beats. At 75 minutes you can let discussion breathe.

1. `[GM]` **Open Checkpoint.** `[PROJECTOR]` shows the title + "read the situation and
   make your decision."
2. `[TABLE]` Read the evidence, **explore the Canvas Classroom pane** (left side of
   the split screen — gradebook, syllabus, modules, announcements, student support),
   discuss, and submit the team decision. *(Private round runs right after on
   CP1 / CP3 / CP4 / CP5.)*
3. `[GM]` When the command center says **"All teams are ready,"** click **Begin
   Discussion** → one discussion question hits the projector. Facilitate it, then
   **Reveal Canvas Strategies** → concrete Canvas moves appear. Facilitate ~2–3 min.
4. `[GM]` **Advance to Next Checkpoint** (or **Proceed to Final** after CP5).

> **Slow-table rule (every checkpoint):** you don't wait forever. When most tables
> are done, click **Lock Checkpoint**; you'll get a confirmation that *names the
> unfinished table* — choose **"Lock … anyway (override)."** Nothing is lost; the
> slow table simply misses that round's decision and rejoins next checkpoint. The
> GM screen shows this hint right in the teaching block.

### ⏱ 0:14–0:26 — Checkpoint 1: First Week (12 min)
- `[GM]` **Open Checkpoint 1.**
- `[TABLE]` Decision — syllabus-vs-Canvas due-date mismatch; up to 3 actions. ~5 min.
- `[TABLE]` **Private round** (pass-the-laptop): confirm allegiance (already known),
  **Claim** a mission if its window is open, then cast a **suspicion vote**. ~4 min.
- `[GM]` **Begin Discussion → Reveal Canvas Strategies** (teaching ~3 min): *where
  does the problem actually live — the student, or the course setup?*
- `[GM]` **Advance to Checkpoint 2.**

### ⏱ 0:26–0:34 — Checkpoint 2: Early Semester (8 min)
- `[GM]` **Open Checkpoint 2.**
- `[TABLE]` Late-add students arrive; onboarding decision (up to 2 actions). **No
  private round this checkpoint.** ~5 min.
- `[GM]` **Begin Discussion → Reveal Canvas Strategies** (teaching ~3 min): build a
  reusable system vs. a one-off scramble.
- `[GM]` **Advance to Checkpoint 3.**

### ⏱ 0:34–0:47 — Checkpoint 3: First Major Assessment (13 min)
- `[GM]` **Open Checkpoint 3.**
- `[TABLE]` Decision — practice-to-assessment alignment; up to 3 actions. ~5 min.
- `[TABLE]` **Private round.** This is a **sabotage window**: a Thunderflunked may
  activate their one-time Emergency Sabotage inside their own turn. ~4 min.
- **Hallway rumor:** if a sabotage fired, `/gm` **Hallway Rumors** shows *"1 rumor
  ready to announce."* `[GM]` **Announce Rumor** → the projector does a full
  **"WHAT YOU'RE HEARING IN THE HALLWAYS…"** takeover. Read it with a little drama,
  then **Dismiss — return the projector.** ~1 min.
- `[GM]` **Begin Discussion → Reveal Canvas Strategies** (teaching ~3 min).
- `[GM]` **Advance to Checkpoint 4.**

### ⏱ 0:47–0:57 — Checkpoint 4: Midterm (10 min)
- `[GM]` **Open Checkpoint 4.**
- `[TABLE]` Prioritize + scale: one **priority** + one **supporting** response.
  *(If a resource is already Critical, "email everyone individually" is correctly
  unavailable — that's the capacity lesson.)* ~5 min.
- `[TABLE]` **Brief private round** (sabotage-only; the last chance to sabotage). ~2 min.
- Announce any new hallway rumor as above (~0–1 min).
- `[GM]` **Begin Discussion → Reveal Canvas Strategies** (teaching ~2–3 min).
- `[GM]` **Advance to Checkpoint 5.**

### ⏱ 0:57–1:09 — Checkpoint 5: Final Stretch (12 min)
- `[GM]` **Open Checkpoint 5.**
- `[TABLE]` Choose one thing to **protect**, one to **repair**, one to intentionally
  **not add**. ~5 min.
- `[TABLE]` **Final private round** (last mission claims + suspicion vote). ~4 min.
- `[GM]` **Begin Discussion → Reveal Canvas Strategies** (teaching ~3 min): finish
  sustainably — protect capacity, don't pile on new scope.
- `[GM]` **Proceed to Final.**

---

## ⏱ 1:09–1:16 — Final accusation, reveal & debrief (7 min)
- `[TABLE]` One last pass-the-laptop: discuss and submit the table's **accusation**
  of who was Thunderflunked. ~3 min.
- `[PROJECTOR]` Final scores/outcomes table across tables.
- `[TABLE]` Each team's screen reveals: **Survived / Did Not Survive**, the four
  resource meters, the **weakest-link explanation** ("one collapsed dimension sinks
  the course"), **what moved the needle** (biggest helpful + costly decision), the
  **Thunderflunked reveal** (and whether the accusation was right), the
  **secret-mission results**, and — if they were sabotaged — a one-line
  **hallway-complication** note. ~2 min.
- `[SAY] DEBRIEF (2 min):` "Thunderflunked couldn't sink the course alone — they
  could only push ideas and pressure. **Good course design isn't knowing who had the
  bad idea; it's having a process that catches the bad idea.** Pick one Canvas
  strategy from today you could use in *your* course Monday."

*(Total ≈ 76 minutes; a brisk room lands closer to 70. The pacing panel on `/gm`
keeps you honest.)*

## RECOVERY TACTICS (keep the room moving)

| Situation | Do this |
|---|---|
| One table is 1–2 min behind on a decision | 60-second verbal warning; let them finish while you prep the teaching question. |
| A table is blocking the whole room | **Lock Checkpoint → "Lock anyway (override)."** It names the table; nothing is lost; they rejoin next checkpoint. |
| A player fumbled a private screen (clicked away / refreshed) | They're safe — refresh re-hides secrets. They tap **Reveal My Mission** again to re-view; an allegiance re-view during setup needs your `/gm` **Reset reveal** for that one player. |
| Table laptop closed / lost | Re-open `/team`, re-enter the **same code + exact team name**; it resumes in place (no duplicate team). |
| You need to stop the room | `[GM]` **Pause room for discussion** → projector shows *Paused*; **Resume** restores the exact state. |
| Discussion is dragging | Time-box: reveal the strategies, name one concrete move, **Advance**. |

## CONTINGENCY — if you're pushing past ~80 minutes

**Do not delete checkpoints or teaching moments.** In priority order:
1. **Time-box discussions** to ~2 min and teaching moments to ~2 min. Saves ~5–8 min.
2. **Shorten decision windows** with a visible countdown. Saves ~3–5 min.
3. **Compress private rounds:** each turn is claim-or-pass + vote, ~30 s, no debate on
   the laptop. Saves ~3–4 min.
4. If still long, facilitate discussion aloud on **CP1, CP3, CP5** only while still
   *showing* all five strategy reveals on the projector. Recovers ~4 min.
5. Hard-stop last resort: **jump to Final after CP4** — but only with the group's
   agreement, and show the CP5 strategies on the projector as a takeaway. This is the
   only option that drops a decision round, so **get buy-in; never do it silently.**

## THREE-PERSON LIVE PILOT CHECKLIST (do this before the real event)

Run one table of ~4–5 (borrow colleagues) end-to-end on real devices. Confirm:

- [ ] Server reachable from a **non-GM laptop** over the room Wi-Fi (LAN IP, not
      127.0.0.1); Windows Firewall isn't blocking Python.
- [ ] Projector text **readable from the back of the room** — teaching question,
      strategy cards, hallway rumor, and final outcomes.
- [ ] The **pass-the-laptop** cadence feels natural; players actually look away.
      Time one private round — ~30–45 s/player is normal.
- [ ] A player can **privately re-view their mission** mid-checkpoint, and a refresh
      doesn't expose anyone's allegiance.
- [ ] Trigger a **sabotage**, then **Announce** the hallway rumor and **Dismiss** it;
      confirm the projector takes over and then restores the prior screen.
- [ ] **Slow-table override:** leave one "player" unfinished, click **Lock
      Checkpoint**, confirm the override names them and proceeds cleanly.
- [ ] **Pause/Resume** mid-checkpoint restores the exact state.
- [ ] Final reveal shows the **weakest-link explanation** and **what-moved-the-needle**
      and reads clearly to a newcomer.
- [ ] Time the whole run; note where minutes went and adjust your time-boxes.
- [ ] Confirm the **GM pacing timer** reflects reality (Start it at 0:00).
