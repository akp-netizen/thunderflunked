# Facilitator Run-of-Show — One Hour

This is the minute-by-minute run sheet. Times are targets; a confident table can
run faster. Everything you control happens on the **GM screen** (`/gm`). Teams
only ever see what you have opened.

**Legend:** [SAY] = read/paraphrase aloud. [DO] = facilitator action. [TABLE] =
what players physically do. [GM] = button on the Game Master screen.
[PROJECTOR] = what the room screen should be showing.

Before players arrive: start the server, open `/gm` and `/projector`, and
**Create New Game**. Have the card decks sorted (Course, Role, Thunderstruck/
Thunderflunked, Secret Mission, and the Resource & Support deck — COURSE TOOL and
STUDENT SUPPORT cards) and the Student Card deck shuffled. Keep the Resource &
Support cards within reach at each table so faculty can consult them during
checkpoints.

---

## 0:00–0:03 — Welcome & framing (3 min)

- [SAY] "You are an instructional team guiding one college course through a
  semester. Your job is to get your students and your course through the term.
  This is about good course-design *process*, not spotting bad students."
- [SAY] "One person at each table is secretly Thunderflunked and wants the course
  to fail — mostly by being persuasive. You will not catch them by vibes. You
  will catch bad ideas with good process."
- [PROJECTOR] Shows "Course Assignments."
- [GM] Read the **game code** to the room.

## 0:03–0:06 — Join & register players (3 min)

- [TABLE] Each team opens `/team`, enters the game code and a team name, chooses
  4/5/6 players, and types player names.
- [TABLE] Each player draws one physical **Role Card** and one physical **Secret
  Mission** (keep missions hidden). Record each player's role on the team device.
- [SAY] "Your Role is a perspective to defend in discussion. It is not a power."

## 0:06–0:09 — Allegiance is assigned (handled in software) (part of above)

- The app has already secretly assigned exactly one Thunderflunked per table.
  Players will privately learn their allegiance during the first Private Player
  Check (after Checkpoint 1). Hand out the physical Thunderstruck / Thunderflunked
  cards at that point, or let the on-screen reveal stand on its own.

## 0:06–0:12 — Course draw, trading, registration (≈6 min)

- [TABLE] Each team draws one physical **Course Card**. Read it — modality,
  assessment pattern, and the two Course Realities differ by course.
- [GM] Click **Open Course Trading**. [PROJECTOR] shows "Course Trading Is Open."
- [TABLE] Teams may negotiate one-for-one physical trades. The software does not
  track trades. (Skip entirely for a single table.)
- [GM] Click **Close Course Trading** when done (about 2 minutes).
- [TABLE] Each team registers the course now in front of them on their device.

## 0:12–0:18 — Student deal, draft, starting conditions, handoff (≈6 min)

- [TABLE] Deal each team **15** physical Student Cards; register those 15.
- [TABLE] Draft exactly **10** for the Starting Roster. The other **5** become
  Rejected Students.
- [DO] Teams read their Starting Conditions (resource meters + a non-blaming
  roster-pattern note). [SAY] "Your roster doesn't decide if you succeed; it
  shapes what this group needs from the course."
- [TABLE] **Rejected-student handoff:** each team passes its 5 Rejected Student
  Cards to the next team and receives 5 from another team (the device tells them
  exactly which, and who to pass to / receive from). Keep received cards separate
  from the roster.

> **Time cut if behind:** have each team draw a pre-sorted 15 instead of dealing;
> skip trading.

---

## 0:18–0:25 — Checkpoint 1: First Week (≈7 min)

- [GM] **Open Checkpoint.** [PROJECTOR] shows "Checkpoint 1 (OPEN)."
- [TABLE] Read the First Week evidence (roster, grades, activity, messages).
  Note the planted detail: the syllabus and Canvas disagree on the due date —
  that is a *course* inconsistency, not only a student mistake.
- [TABLE] Choose up to **3** actions and submit. Discuss as a team first.
- [DO] Teams read the narrative consequence, then run the **Private Player Check**
  (see the Private Check block below).
- [GM] **Lock Checkpoint**, then **Advance to Next Checkpoint.**

## 0:25–0:31 — Checkpoint 2: Early Semester / Late Adds (≈6 min)

- [GM] **Open Checkpoint.**
- [TABLE] 2–4 of the received students join the roster. Read their full cards.
- [SAY] "New students are not damage. The question is whether your course can
  absorb normal enrollment change."
- [TABLE] Choose up to **2** onboarding actions and submit.
- [DO] Teams read the consequence, then run the **Private Player Check.** The
  Thunderflunked is offered Emergency Sabotage during their own private turn
  (Checkpoints 2–4) — no separate step, and it does not out them.
- [GM] **Lock**, then **Advance.**

## 0:31–0:38 — Checkpoint 3: First Major Assessment (≈7 min)

- [GM] **Open Checkpoint.**
- [TABLE] Read the results. Class average ~71%. Most of the loss is in one
  higher-order skill (justification), but highly engaged students struggled too,
  and practice asked students to *identify* while the assessment asked them to
  *justify.* Multiple causes at once — investigate before deciding.
- [TABLE] Choose up to **3** actions and submit.
- [DO] Teams read the consequence, then run the **Private Player Check.** The
  Thunderflunked may activate Emergency Sabotage during their private turn.
- [GM] **Lock**, then **Advance.**

## 0:38–0:45 — Checkpoint 4: Midterm (≈7 min)

- [GM] **Open Checkpoint.**
- [TABLE] More needs than they can perfectly address: a persistence-risk student,
  a shared misconception, a grading backlog, a feedback-visibility problem, and a
  callback showing an earlier whole-class move already helped.
- [TABLE] Choose **one priority** response and **one supporting** response.
- [SAY] "Prioritize and scale. You cannot do everything well."
- [DO] Teams read the consequence, then run the **Private Player Check.** This is
  the Thunderflunked's **last chance** to use Emergency Sabotage (during their
  private turn) — it expires after this checkpoint.
- [GM] **Lock**, then **Advance.**

## 0:45–0:51 — Checkpoint 5: Final Stretch (≈6 min)

- [GM] **Open Checkpoint.**
- [TABLE] Choose **one thing to Protect**, **one thing to Repair**, and **one
  thing you will intentionally NOT add.** Submit.
- [SAY] "Finish sustainably and accurately. A student failing is not the same as
  the course failing."
- [DO] Run the final **Private Player Check.**
- [GM] **Lock Checkpoint.**

## 0:51–0:55 — Final Accusation & Reveal (≈4 min)

- [GM] **Proceed to Final Accusation & Outcome.**
- [TABLE] Read the vague Final Suspicion Report, discuss briefly, and lock in one
  accusation of who was Thunderflunked (aim for consensus).
- [DO] The screen reveals the Thunderflunked player, whether the team was right,
  the course outcome (Survived / Did Not Survive), final scores, and the debrief.
- [PROJECTOR] Now shows each team's course, final score, and outcome.

## 0:55–1:00 — Debrief (5–6 min)

- [SAY] Read the closing lines with the room:
  - "Thunderflunked did not have the power to destroy your course alone."
  - "They could suggest ideas, push priorities, and create pressure."
  - "Your team's process determined which ideas became decisions."
  - "**Good course design is not about knowing who has the bad idea. It is about
    having a process that catches the bad idea.**"
- [SAY] Ask each table: which single idea almost derailed you, and what in your
  process (a role's challenge, the evidence, a pause) caught it or missed it?

---

## The Private Player Check (after every checkpoint, CP1–CP5)

Runs on the team's one device, one player at a time.

1. The screen names the player to receive the device; everyone else looks away.
2. That player taps **I'm Ready**, then privately sees: their **allegiance**
   (first time only), a **Secret Mission** claim prompt (refers to their physical
   mission card), (Checkpoints 2–4, Thunderflunked only) an **Emergency Sabotage**
   option, and a **Suspicion Vote** for one other player.
3. The device returns to a neutral "pass to the next player" screen.
4. After everyone has gone, the team sees a vague report: suspicion is
   *scattered*, *concentrated*, or *divided* — never names, counts, or votes.

Keep this moving: target ~60–90 seconds per player. The Secret Mission prompt is
just "did your physical card's condition happen — Claim or Not Yet"; it is a small
end-of-game bonus and does not decide the winner.

---

## Emergency 45-minute cut (if the live session is running long)

Preserve the learning arc with these exact cuts (see also the report's §9):

1. **Skip Course Trading** — draw and register courses directly (saves ~4 min).
2. **Pre-deal rosters** — hand each team a pre-sorted 15; still draft 10 (saves ~3 min).
3. **Run the full private round only at CP1, CP3, and CP5** — at CP2 and CP4 skip
   the vote/mission pass (the Thunderflunked can still save sabotage for CP3)
   (saves ~4–6 min).
4. **Checkpoint 2 as a 3-minute discussion** — read the intake evidence aloud and
   take a show-of-hands onboarding choice, then submit for each team (saves ~3 min).
5. **Tighten the debrief to 3 minutes** — read the four closing lines and take one
   table's example.

This keeps all five checkpoints, both private-check bookends, allegiance, one
sabotage window, the accusation, the outcome, and the core debrief intact.
